# QURAN BOT — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how QURAN BOT handles information when you use the application on Discord.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, QURAN BOT may process the following information:

- Discord Server IDs (Guild IDs).
- Discord User IDs when users interact with bot commands.
- Channel IDs, including voice and text channel IDs required for bot functionality.
- Server settings and preferences configured by server administrators.
- Command interaction information needed to respond to requests.

We only intend to process information necessary to operate and maintain the application's features.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- Provide Quran recitation and voice playback.
- Deliver Adhkar and scheduled Islamic reminders.
- Remember server-specific settings and preferences.
- Respond to Discord commands and interactions.
- Troubleshoot errors and maintain application reliability.
- Protect the application against abuse.

## 3. Voice and Audio Information

QURAN BOT connects to Discord voice channels to provide Quran recitation.

The application is designed to stream Quran audio. It is not intended to record or store users' conversations or personal voice communications.

## 4. Message Content

QURAN BOT does not need to collect or store ordinary Discord message content for its core Quran recitation and Adhkar features.

However, information included in commands or interactions may be processed when necessary to provide requested functionality.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord and infrastructure or service providers necessary to operate the application.

We do not intentionally share user information with unrelated third parties.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's features, maintain server settings, and meet legitimate operational requirements.

Server administrators may request deletion of their server's stored configuration and associated data by contacting us.

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable period, subject to applicable requirements.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by QURAN BOT against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

QURAN BOT is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from users.

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
